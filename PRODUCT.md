# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js. The deployment target and backend architecture are undecided. The initial product surface is a landing page; dynamic backend capabilities will be added later.

## Users

The primary users are proven builders and founders in technology who demonstrate unusually high agency. They are familiar with what is happening across the technology landscape, keep going without needing external pressure, and have already shipped something meaningful rather than only aspiring, talking, or presenting potential.

Builders and founders are the two initial audience segments because they are the clearest fit for the community and its future business model.

## Product Purpose

First Artifact is a selective club for highly driven builders and founders. It exists to create a dense community of people whose defining trait is obsession expressed through action: they build, ship, learn, and persist.

The community should help members meet peers of a similarly high standard, make meaningful progress, and participate in experiences that feel fun rather than obligatory. Its working model for fun is:

`Fun ≈ Agency × Progress × Optimal Challenge × Feedback`

Success means maintaining an unusually engaged membership, creating useful relationships among proven builders and founders, and building a reputation through the quality of the community and its events.

## Positioning

First Artifact is not a broad networking group or another generic builder community. Its distinction is a deliberately high and clearly enforced acceptance bar based on demonstrated work, sustained obsession, and high-agency behavior.

Talent alone is common; obsession is rare. The product belief is that obsession beats talent because obsessed people continue acting, improving, and shipping. Membership is therefore earned through evidence of building, not interest, aspiration, credentials, or talk.

Exclusivity is functional: it protects member quality, raises trust inside the group, and creates a credible external signal that access is scarce and valuable.

## Operating Context

The initial public experience is a landing page that introduces the club, establishes its standard, and gives qualified builders and founders a way to pursue membership.

The broader community may include networking events, hacker houses, hackathons, and buildathons. These experiences are intended to deepen relationships, sustain engagement, create visible progress, and build the club's reputation.

Potential monetization directions include community-as-a-service and carefully managed access for venture capital firms seeking relationships with members. The exact model, boundaries, and member protections are undecided.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Admission must use a strict, explicit filter. Applicants must have already built or shipped something good; the bar must not loosen into acceptance based on potential alone.
- The community must optimize for active, engaged members rather than membership volume.
- Community participation must feel enjoyable and worthwhile, not like another recurring obligation.
- The subject matter may include AI but is not restricted to AI; members should be deeply engaged with technology more broadly.
- Builders and founders are the initial member segments.
- Events are part of the intended community model, including hacker houses, hackathons, buildathons, and networking experiences.
- Dynamic application, membership, event, or community features may be added after the initial landing page.
- The exact admission rubric, application process, community platform, moderation model, pricing, deployment target, backend architecture, and monetization model remain open decisions.

- Core language: cracked builders and founders, high agency, obsessed, proven builders, and people who ship.
- Voice should be direct, energetic, selective, and unambiguous about the standard.

- The recurring product belief is: obsession beats talent.

## Product Principles

1. **Evidence over potential.** Judge membership by what someone has built, shipped, and sustained—not by aspiration, credentials, or self-description.
2. **Protect the density.** A smaller group of exceptional, active people is more valuable than a large, weakly engaged membership.
3. **Agency is the engine.** Create conditions where members initiate, contribute, and keep moving without being managed into participation.
4. **Make progress enjoyable.** Use meaningful challenge, fast feedback, visible progress, and member autonomy to make participation intrinsically fun.
5. **Exclusivity must earn its value.** Scarcity should result from a real, consistently enforced standard and improve the member experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The landing page and future product experiences must be fully accessible. Specific conformance targets and testing requirements are not yet defined; future implementation should establish them explicitly rather than treating accessibility as a visual-only check.

Typography implementation must preserve readable body text and usable fallback fonts. Because the supplied GoCake release has a limited glyph set, Source Sans 3 or another approved accessible fallback must cover unsupported characters and future localization.
